## Overview

At WREXT, we want you to be completely satisfied with your subscription. This document outlines our policies for cancellations, refunds, and subscription changes.

**Key Points:**
- ✅ Cancel anytime, no questions asked
- ✅ Pro-rated refunds available within 14 days (subject to conditions)
- ✅ Free trial period with no payment required
- ✅ Flexible plan changes (upgrades and downgrades)
- ✅ Fair and transparent refund process

**Payment Processor:** All payments are processed by **LemonSqueezy**, our merchant of record. Refunds are issued through the same payment method you used for the original purchase.

## Cancellation Policy

### You Can Cancel Anytime

WREXT subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, with no cancellation fees or penalties.

### How to Cancel

**Option 1: Self-Service Cancellation (Recommended)**

1. Log in to your WREXT account
2. Go to **Billing Dashboard** (or **Settings → Billing**)
3. Click **Manage Subscription**
4. Click **Cancel Subscription**
5. Confirm cancellation

**Option 2: Email Request**

Email: support@wrext.com
Subject: "Cancel Subscription - [Your Email]"

**Response time:** Within 1 business day

### What Happens When You Cancel?

**Immediate Effects:**
- ✅ Your subscription will be marked as "Cancelled"
- ✅ You'll receive a cancellation confirmation email
- ✅ No further charges will be made

**Until End of Billing Period:**
- ✅ You retain **full access** to all paid features until the end of your current billing period
- ✅ You can continue using WREXT normally
- ✅ You can still upgrade or reactivate your subscription

**After Billing Period Ends:**
- ❌ Paid features will be disabled
- ✅ Your account remains active (converted to Free plan)
- ✅ Your data is retained (see [Data Retention](#data-retention))
- ✅ You can resubscribe anytime to regain access

**Example:**
- You subscribe to the **Pro Plan** on January 1st (monthly billing)
- You cancel on January 15th
- You have full access until **January 31st** (end of billing period)
- On February 1st, your account converts to the **Free Plan**
- No charge on February 1st (subscription already cancelled)

### Cancellation Does NOT Trigger Automatic Refund

**Important:** Cancelling your subscription does **NOT** automatically issue a refund. To request a refund, see [How to Request a Refund](#how-to-request-a-refund).

**Why?**
- You retain access until the end of your billing period
- Most users cancel mid-cycle and want to keep using the service
- Refunds are available within 14 days if you meet eligibility criteria (see below)

## Refund Policy

### 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a **14-day money-back guarantee** for all subscription plans, subject to the conditions below.

### Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a **full or pro-rated refund** if:

1. ✅ **Within 14 days** of your most recent charge
2. ✅ **Minimal usage** of paid features (see criteria below)
3. ✅ **No prior refund** for the same subscription (one refund per customer per plan)
4. ✅ **No abuse or violation** of Terms of Service

### Refund Types

#### 1. **Full Refund (100%)**

**Eligibility:**
- Requested within **14 days** of initial subscription charge
- **Minimal or no usage** of paid features (less than 10% of limits)
- First-time subscriber or significant service issue

**Examples:**
- Subscribed yesterday, used 2 out of 100 monthly content generations → Full refund
- Technical issue prevented service usage → Full refund
- Duplicate charge or billing error → Full refund

#### 2. **Pro-Rated Refund (Partial)**

**Eligibility:**
- Requested within **14 days** of most recent charge
- **Moderate usage** of paid features (10%-50% of limits)
- No full refund eligibility

**Calculation:**
```
Refund Amount = (Subscription Price) × (Days Remaining / Days in Billing Period)
```

**Example (Monthly Subscription):**
- Plan: Pro Plan - $29/month
- Billing Period: January 1 - January 31 (31 days)
- Refund Requested: January 10 (21 days remaining)
- Refund Amount: $29 × (21 / 31) = **$19.65**

**Example (Annual Subscription):**
- Plan: Pro Plan - $290/year
- Billing Period: January 1 - December 31 (365 days)
- Refund Requested: January 15 (350 days remaining)
- Refund Amount: $290 × (350 / 365) = **$278.08**

#### 3. **No Refund (0%)**

**Reasons for refund denial:**
- ❌ Requested **more than 14 days** after charge
- ❌ **High usage** of paid features (over 50% of limits)
- ❌ **Second refund request** for the same plan
- ❌ **Abuse or violation** of Terms of Service (e.g., fraud, excessive API usage, data scraping)
- ❌ **Chargebacks initiated** (see below)

### Usage Thresholds for Refunds

We assess usage of paid features to determine refund eligibility:

| Feature | Minimal Usage (Full Refund) | Moderate Usage (Pro-Rated) | High Usage (No Refund) |
|---------|---------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| **Content Generations** | <10% of monthly limit | 10%-50% of limit | >50% of limit |
| **API Requests** | <10% of monthly limit | 10%-50% of limit | >50% of limit |
| **Storage** | <10% of plan allowance | 10%-50% of allowance | >50% of allowance |
| **Team Members** | <2 users added | 2-5 users added | >5 users added |

**Note:** Thresholds are guidelines. We review each request individually and may grant exceptions for legitimate service issues.

### Refund Exceptions

**We DO offer refunds for:**
- ✅ Service outages or technical issues (beyond our control)
- ✅ Billing errors or duplicate charges (always eligible)
- ✅ Accidental plan upgrades (within 48 hours)
- ✅ Dissatisfaction with service quality (within 14 days, minimal usage)

**We do NOT offer refunds for:**
- ❌ Change of mind after 14 days
- ❌ Feature requests not met (unless service was misrepresented)
- ❌ User error or lack of understanding (we'll provide support instead)
- ❌ Third-party integrations or external service issues
- ❌ Chargebacks (dispute through payment processor instead)

## Trial Period Policy

### Free Trial Terms

All new users receive a **14-day free trial** of paid plans (Pro, Enterprise).

**Trial Terms:**
- ✅ **No credit card required** to start trial
- ✅ **Full access** to all plan features
- ✅ **14 days** from trial activation
- ✅ **No automatic charge** at trial end (must manually subscribe)

### Trial Conversion

**What Happens at Trial End:**

1. **If you subscribe during trial:**
   - You are charged for your selected plan
   - 14-day money-back guarantee applies (from subscription date, not trial start)

2. **If you do NOT subscribe:**
   - Trial expires automatically
   - Account converts to **Free Plan**
   - No charge applied
   - You can subscribe anytime to regain access

### Trial Cancellation

**Can I cancel my trial early?**
- Yes, trials can be cancelled anytime
- No charge if you cancel before trial ends
- Account converts to Free Plan immediately (or at trial end)

**How to cancel trial:**
- Same process as regular cancellation (see [How to Cancel](#how-to-cancel))

### Trial Eligibility

**One trial per customer:**
- ❌ Cannot create multiple accounts for repeat trials
- ✅ Can try different plans (if you haven't trialed that specific plan before)
- ❌ Abuse of trial system may result in account suspension

## Subscription Changes

### Upgrading Your Plan

**How to upgrade:**
1. Go to **Billing Dashboard** → **Change Plan**
2. Select higher-tier plan (e.g., Starter → Pro)
3. Confirm upgrade

**Billing for upgrades:**
- **Pro-rated charge** for the higher-tier plan
- Credit applied for unused time on current plan
- New billing cycle starts immediately

**Example:**
- Current Plan: **Starter Plan** ($9/month)
- Upgrade Date: **Day 15** of billing cycle (15 days remaining)
- New Plan: **Pro Plan** ($29/month)
- **Charge Today:** $29 - ($9 × 15/30) = $29 - $4.50 = **$24.50**
- **Next Charge:** In 15 days (pro-rated to align with original billing date)

**Access:**
- ✅ Immediate access to new plan features
- ✅ Higher limits and features active instantly

### Downgrading Your Plan

**How to downgrade:**
1. Go to **Billing Dashboard** → **Change Plan**
2. Select lower-tier plan (e.g., Pro → Starter)
3. Confirm downgrade

**Billing for downgrades:**
- **No immediate charge or refund**
- Downgrade takes effect at **end of current billing period**
- You keep current plan access until then
- Next charge will be for the lower-tier plan

**Example:**
- Current Plan: **Pro Plan** ($29/month)
- Downgrade Date: **Day 10** of billing cycle (20 days remaining)
- New Plan: **Starter Plan** ($9/month)
- **Today:** No charge, Pro Plan access continues
- **Day 30:** Pro Plan access ends, Starter Plan begins
- **Day 30 Charge:** $9/month (Starter Plan price)

**Access:**
- ✅ Current plan features until end of billing period
- ❌ Features disabled at downgrade date if exceeding new plan limits
- ⚠️ **Warning:** If you exceed new plan limits (e.g., storage, team members), you must reduce usage before downgrade takes effect

### Switching Billing Frequency

**Monthly to Annual (or vice versa):**
- Treated as a plan change (pro-rated credit/charge)
- New billing cycle starts immediately

**Example (Monthly → Annual):**
- Current Plan: Pro Plan ($29/month)
- Switch to: Pro Plan ($290/year, ~$24.17/month)
- **Savings:** ~$58/year (2 months free)
- **Charge Today:** $290 - (unused monthly credit)

## How to Request a Refund

### Step 1: Cancel Your Subscription

**Important:** You must cancel your subscription **before** or **during** the refund request process. Otherwise, you will continue to be charged.

See [How to Cancel](#how-to-cancel) for instructions.

### Step 2: Submit Refund Request

**Option 1: Email Request (Recommended)**

**Email:** billing@wrext.com
**Subject:** "Refund Request - [Your Email]"

**Include in your email:**
- Your account email address
- Subscription plan name
- Reason for refund request (optional but helpful)
- Date of most recent charge
- Preferred refund method (usually same as payment method)

**Option 2: LemonSqueezy Customer Portal**

1. Log in to WREXT
2. Go to **Billing Dashboard**
3. Click **Manage Billing** (opens LemonSqueezy Customer Portal)
4. Click **Request Refund** (if available within refund window)
5. Follow prompts

### Step 3: Review & Approval

**Our Process:**

1. **Receive Request** (within 24 hours)
   - Automated confirmation email sent

2. **Review Eligibility** (1-2 business days)
   - Check refund window (14 days)
   - Review usage data
   - Assess refund type (full, pro-rated, none)

3. **Approval or Denial** (email notification)
   - **Approved:** Refund amount and processing time
   - **Denied:** Reason for denial and options (e.g., extend subscription instead)
   - **Partial:** Pro-rated refund amount and calculation

4. **Issue Refund** (3-10 business days)
   - Refund processed through LemonSqueezy
   - Confirmation email from LemonSqueezy

### Step 4: Receive Refund

**Refund Method:**
- Same payment method used for original purchase
- Credit/debit card: 3-10 business days (depends on bank)
- PayPal: 2-5 business days
- Bank transfer: 5-10 business days

**Note:** Refund timing depends on your payment provider (bank, PayPal, etc.). WREXT and LemonSqueezy process refunds within 1-2 business days, but your bank may take additional time to credit your account.

## Exceptions & Special Cases

### Billing Errors

**Examples:**
- Duplicate charges
- Incorrect amount charged
- Charged after cancellation

**Resolution:**
- ✅ **Automatic full refund** (no questions asked)
- ✅ Processed within **24-48 hours**
- ✅ No impact on future refund eligibility

**How to report:**
Email: billing@wrext.com
Subject: "Billing Error - [Your Email]"

### Service Outages

**Major Outages (>24 hours):**
- ✅ Pro-rated credit or refund for downtime
- ✅ Automatic compensation (no request needed)
- ✅ Email notification with credit details

**Minor Outages (<24 hours):**
- ✅ Service credits may be issued (case-by-case)
- ✅ Request via support@wrext.com

### Chargebacks

**Important:** Do **NOT** initiate a chargeback without contacting us first.

**Why?**
- Chargebacks trigger **immediate account suspension**
- Chargeback fees ($15-$25) may be charged to you
- Refund requests through proper channels are faster and easier

**If you initiate a chargeback:**
1. Your account will be **suspended immediately**
2. We will **contest the chargeback** with evidence (usage logs, terms acceptance)
3. If chargeback is successful, your account will be **permanently banned**
4. Chargeback fees may be recovered through legal means

**Proper process:**
- Contact us first: billing@wrext.com
- We will resolve billing disputes within 24-48 hours
- Chargebacks should be a last resort (after exhausting support channels)

### Subscription Disputes

**If you disagree with a refund decision:**

1. **Reply to the refund denial email** with additional context
2. **Escalate to management:** billing-disputes@wrext.com
3. **Response time:** 2-3 business days
4. **Final decision:** Made by billing manager

**We're reasonable:**
- If there was a legitimate service issue, we'll work with you
- Exceptional circumstances may warrant exceptions to policy
- We value long-term customer relationships over one-time revenue

## Data Retention After Cancellation

### What Happens to Your Data?

**Immediately after cancellation:**
- ✅ Your data is **retained** (not deleted)
- ✅ Account converts to **Free Plan**
- ✅ You can resubscribe anytime to regain access

**Data retention period:**
- **30 days:** Full data retention (can resubscribe without data loss)
- **30-90 days:** Data retained but may be archived
- **90+ days:** Data may be deleted (compliance with retention policies)

**Permanent deletion:**
- You must **request account deletion** separately (see [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_PAYMENTS.md))
- Deletion process: 7-day grace period, then permanent deletion
- Financial records retained for 7 years (anonymized, for tax compliance)
